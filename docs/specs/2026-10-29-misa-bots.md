# Misa's bots (MidariBread), 2026-10-29

Owner request: the player Misa (MidariBread) gets her own bots. She is not technical and plays from a normal client;
everything she does is a whispered order. A German PDF guide explains it to her.

Builds on the guest bot commit (35c3fbc, branch `guest-bot`): `mcbots-worker-5`, `GUEST_BOTS`, order outcomes
whispered back, `gib`. That commit had `bot22`; this replaces it before it ever ran on the lab.

## Decisions (owner, 2026-10-29)

1. Her helper is called **MisaBot** (a real name, not `botNN`). BotGate (`minecraft/botgate/BotGate.java`) and the
   mcbots `NAME_RE` (`app/bots.js`) admit exactly this one extra name. Deploying restarts Velocity (all players are
   kicked once); ship it when nobody plays.
2. Misa has **her own trading hall** (two floors, sticks on one side, villagers on the other). Its coordinates are not
   known yet: they come when Misa is online. The trader is built and tested now and switched on by configuration later.
3. "Bau das ab" means **remove everything between two marked corners, built blocks too**, except containers, and only
   inside Misa's own base area. Never in the trading hall, never in anyone else's protected area.
4. The trader runs **three times a week: Monday, Wednesday, Saturday from 18:00 (Europe/Vienna) for about one hour**,
   then stops by itself.

## Parts

### A. Names and worker

- `mcbots-worker-5` runs `MisaBot,bot23` (two bots in one process like worker-2). `bot23` is the trader. Both are in
  `GUEST_BOTS`, both obey Misa and the owner (`OWNER_UUIDS` of worker-5 unchanged).
- BotGate pattern `^(bot[0-9]{1,2}|MisaBot)$`; same for `NAME_RE`. Case as written (Minecraft names are
  case-insensitive for login, so the check must stay exact or case-insensitive in both places alike; builder's call,
  but consistent).

### B. Protected areas with an owner

- `PROTECTED_AREAS` boxes may name bots that are allowed inside: `x1,z1,x2,z2@MisaBot` (several: `@a+b`). A bot uses only
  the areas that do not name it; the hub and the workers apply this per bot. Areas without `@` stay as they are.
- Misa's base box (already in the list: `112,368,272,592`) gets `@MisaBot`. Her trading hall, once known, is added
  **without** `@`, so no bot ever digs or places there (MisaBot included; nested inside her base is fine: a block is
  protected for a bot if any area that does not name the bot contains it).

### C. Misa's in-game orders (MisaBot)

New words, added to `owner.translate` and the help text (keep the help whisper within 100 characters):

| Order | Effect |
|---|---|
| `ecke` | Remembers the block she stands on as corner 1, the next `ecke` as corner 2 (then 1 again). Reply `ok: Ecke 1: x y z`. Corners belong to the bot, per player. |
| `abbau` | Removes every block between the two corners (all solid blocks and plants, built ones too) except containers and their contents (chests, barrels, shulker boxes, furnaces, hoppers ...). Refused with a reason unless the whole box lies inside an area that names this bot and touches no area that does not. Size limit 32 x 32 x 24; bigger boxes are refused. Work top down; the bot keeps what drops, `gib` hands it over. |
| `bau <name>` | Builds the schematic `<name>.litematic` (from the schematics folder, uploaded by the owner on the dashboard) with its origin on the block she stands on, rotation 0. Optional `bau <name> 90` / `180` / `270`. Materials come from the containers within 8 blocks of where she stands when she gives the order (found at order time). Same area rule as `abbau` for every block it places or clears. |

Outcomes come back as whispers (already built). Unknown schematic name: `geht nicht: kenne <name> nicht`.

### D. Trader (bot23)

- `trade.js` takes its hall from the job's arguments instead of constants: a box (the hall), the stick containers
  (or "every container in the box that is not a store"), and the store containers for emeralds. Without arguments it
  keeps the owner's hall exactly as today.
- Misa's hall geometry is configuration: env `TRADE_HALLS` on the hub (JSON or a compact string, builder's call),
  `misa` = box + stores, validated like other job arguments.
- Schedule on the hub: env `TRADE_SCHEDULE`, e.g. `bot23=misa@mon,wed,sat/18:00+60`: at the start time the hub gives
  the bot a `trade` job for that hall and stops it after the minutes given; a restart inside the window resumes it.
  Times in Europe/Vienna. Empty or a hall that is not configured: nothing runs (that is the state until Misa's hall
  is known).
- The trader never digs (the hall is protected for everyone anyway).

### E. Guide for Misa

German PDF, written for a player who has never used bots: what MisaBot and the trader are, the whisper orders with
examples, how to mark corners, how to get a schematic built (send the file to the owner, put materials in a chest
next to the spot), what the bots will not do (her trading hall, other bases, the owner's chests), what the replies
mean, what to do when the bot is stuck or dead (`status`, `komm`, tell the owner). Rendered with headless Chromium
from HTML; source kept in `docs/guides/`.
